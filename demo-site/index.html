Trail-tested gear for the river valley

Riverbend Supply Co. is a small family outfitter. We stock packs, paddles and camp kitchen gear chosen by guides who run the river every week.

Weekend pickup service

Order by Thursday noon and collect your gear Friday after work at our Mill Street store. Returns are due Monday morning.

Go to the newsletter form to hear about stock arrivals.

Guided trips

Our guides run beginner paddles every Saturday morning, April to October. Equipment and a riverside lunch are included in the price.
